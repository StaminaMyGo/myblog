## 二、英文感想全文（polished American English）

Good evening. I'd like to talk about my thoughts after playing through the classic, well-known work *Witch on the Holy Night*, created by TYPE-MOON (型月), a Japanese company that has grown into a huge empire spanning CG, anime, manga, light novels, and mobile games. What impressed me most were the visuals and special effects—they were unbelievable and brilliant. I've never forgotten what I saw.

Through their work, I first got a strong sense of what it means to love characters and a work—what we call "character love" (角色爱) in Chinese—even though the plot is simple: it's about three people involved with magic, a magician, and the pursuit of power.

One of my favorite characters is the male lead, Soujuurou Shizuki (静希草十郎). His personality is unique: he always keeps a calm expression, solves problems calmly, and takes responsibility for the work and tasks he undertakes. That's the key reason his personality charms the two main female characters, and I really enjoy watching their interactions.

Besides, the soundtrack is awesome because it fits the tone, background, and themes of the story perfectly. It's so good that I listen to every track in my daily routine, especially while working or studying.

Last but not least, if I had the chance to introduce this work to people interested in games or visual novels, I'd strongly recommend *Witch on the Holy Night* as the best choice for getting into the world of visual novels/gal games. I'd even call it the best work I've ever experienced.

To sum up, this work is an essential achievement in visual novels. It has no 18+ or sexual content, so it feels pure and charming. The storytelling lets you forget all the troubles and distractions of reality, and you can completely immerse yourself in this world as if you really lived there.

Thank you—that's all.
