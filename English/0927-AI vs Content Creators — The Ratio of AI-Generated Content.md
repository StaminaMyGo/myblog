## 板块四：5 个真实场景例句

### 例句 1（讨论AI取代创作者）

> **Well...** I dunno about you, but I'm kinda worried that AI's gonna _take over_ (take over) content creation. Like, if AI can write articles and make art for _cheap_ (cheap), why would anyone hire a human?

**连读提示**：take over 中 /k/ 弱读；kinda = kind of

---

### 例句 2（描述AI作为工具）

> **You know what?** I think people are overreacting. AI's just a _tool_ (tool), right? It's like Photoshop or a word processor. It _helps_ (helps) you create faster, but you still need the _human touch_ (human touch).

**连读提示**：human touch 中 /m/ 与 /t/ 连读；helps you 中 /s/ 与 /j/ 连读

---

### 例句 3（讨论内容质量）

> **I mean...** The problem is when people _cut corners_ (cut corners) and just pump out AI content without checking it. The _quality_ (quality) drops, and everything starts to sound the same. _Kinda_ (kinda) scary, honestly.

**连读提示**：cut corners 中 /t/ 不完全爆破；kinda = kind of

---

### 例句 4（描述创作者的适应）

> **Honestly?** I think it's _adapt or die_ (adapt or die) for creators. You can either fight AI or learn to use it. The ones who _jump on the bandwagon_ (jump on the bandwagon) and figure out how to use AI as a tool? They're gonna be fine.

**连读提示**：adapt or 中 /t/ 闪音；jump on 中 /p/ 与 /ɒ/ 连读

---

### 例句 5（总结看法）

> **Look...** AI is a _double-edged sword_ (double-edged sword), no doubt. But there's a _silver lining_ (silver lining)—it takes care of the boring stuff, so creators can focus on the _creative_ (creative) work. _Wanna_ (wanna) know my take? Use AI, but don't let it use you.

**连读提示**：double-edged 中 /l/ 与 /d/ 连读；wanna = want to

## 板块五：完整口语表达模板（约 5 分钟 / 650 词）

### 口语主题：AI vs Content Creators — The Ratio of AI-Generated Content

**完整口语文稿：**

Hey, what's up everyone. So today I **wanna** talk about something that's been on everyone's mind lately—**AI vs content creators, and the ratio of AI-generated content**. Yeah, I know, it's a hot topic, but hear me out, 'cause I think there's a lot more nuance here than people realize.

So, let's start with the numbers. I read somewhere that like, 90% of online **content** is gonna be **AI-generated** by next year. Ninety percent! That's crazy. Think about that—nine out of ten articles you read, nine out of ten images you see, nine out of ten **videos** you watch? They could be made by **AI**. Not by a person. By a machine.

And I know what you're thinking—"Oh no, **AI** is gonna **take over** everything. **Content creators** are doomed. The **human touch** is gone forever." And yeah, that's a valid concern. But I think we're missing the bigger picture here.

Because here's the thing—**AI** is a **tool**. Just like a camera is a **tool**. Just like a word processor is a **tool**. Just like Photoshop is a **tool**. And tools don't replace creators—they empower them. Think about it: when Photoshop came out, did photographers disappear? No. They learned to use it. When digital cameras came out, did film photographers go extinct? Some did, but the ones who **adapted**? They thrived. That's how technology works. It's **adapt or die**.

But I will say, there's a problem. And the problem is **quality**. Because right now, a lot of people are using **AI** to **cut corners**. They're pumping out **AI-generated** **content** as fast as they can, without checking it, without editing it, without adding any **original** thought. And the result? Everything sounds the same. Every article has that generic **AI** voice. Every image has that uncanny **AI** look. It's like, you can spot **AI content** from a mile away now. It's lost that **human touch**, that spark, that thing that makes you go, "Wow, someone really put their heart into this."

And that's the danger, right? If the **ratio** of **AI-generated** **content** keeps going up, and the **quality** keeps going down, we're gonna end up with this internet full of generic, soulless **content**. Nothing **original**, nothing surprising, nothing that makes you feel something. And that's a bummer, man. Because the best **content**—the stuff that sticks with you, that changes how you think—that comes from people. From real **human** experiences, real emotions, real perspectives. **AI** can mimic that, but it can't truly create it. Not yet, anyway.

But here's the **silver lining**. I think **AI** is actually gonna make **human-created** **content** more valuable. Because as the internet gets flooded with **AI** stuff, people are gonna start craving the real thing. They're gonna want **content** that has personality, that has a voice, that has that **human touch**. And **creators** who can deliver that? They're gonna stand out. They're gonna be worth more, not less. Because in a world of generic **AI** **content**, authenticity is a superpower.

I also think **AI** is gonna free up **creators** to do more **creative** work. Think about it—right now, a lot of a **creator's** time is spent on boring stuff. Research, first drafts, basic editing, formatting. **AI** can handle all that. It can do the grunt work in seconds. And that means **creators** can spend more time on the stuff that actually matters—the big ideas, the storytelling, the **creative** decisions, the personal touch. **AI** takes care of the boring, so humans can focus on the brilliant. That's a **game-changer**, man.

I've seen this with my **creator** friends. One of them writes a newsletter. She used to spend like four hours on each issue—researching, drafting, editing. Now she uses **AI** to help with the research and the first draft, and she spends her time on the voice, the perspective, the personal stories. And her newsletter? It's better than ever. Because she's not wasting time on the mechanical stuff. She's focusing on what makes her **content** unique. That's how you use **AI** right.

But I will say, there's a line. And the line is this—**AI** should help you create, not create for you. If you're just hitting "generate" and publishing whatever comes out? That's not **content creation**. That's just pressing a button. But if you're using **AI** as a **tool**—as an assistant, as a brainstorming partner, as a way to speed up the boring parts—then you're still a **creator**. You're still adding value. You're still bringing that **human touch**.

And honestly? I think that's the **future**. The **ratio** of **AI-generated** **content** is gonna keep going up. There's no stopping that. But the best **content**? The stuff that people actually care about? That's always gonna need a **human**. Because at the end of the day, people connect with people. Not with machines.

So, if you're a **content creator** and you're worried about **AI**? Don't be. **Adapt**. Learn to use it. Let it handle the boring stuff, and focus on what makes you unique. Your voice, your perspective, your stories. That's the stuff **AI** can't replicate. That's your superpower.

Alright, that's my take. It's a **double-edged sword**, but there's a **silver lining**. Use **AI**, but don't let it use you. Catch you next time.
