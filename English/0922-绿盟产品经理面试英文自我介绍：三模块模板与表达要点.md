# 绿盟产品经理面试英文自我介绍：三模块模板与表达要点

> 适用场景：绿盟科技产品经理英文面试 / 2.5–3 分钟自我介绍 / 中英文简历配套表达
>
> 核心原则：**让面试官听到"你懂技术、能沟通、能推进项目，并且对 AI 安全方向有真实兴趣"**——背诵不是目的，表达出的信号才是。

---

## 一、三模块结构总览

| 模块 | 定位 | 覆盖信息 | 时长占比 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Part 1: Personal Introduction** | 建立可信身份 | 专业背景 + 技术栈 + 竞赛 + 特长 + 好奇心 | 约 40% |
| **Part 2: Project Experience** | 证明执行与协作 | 校企实习 + AI 前端项目全流程 + 团队管理 + 结果导向 | 约 30% |
| **Part 3: Industry Frontier & Career Direction** | 展示功课与野心 | 绿盟双轮驱动 + AI 安全前沿 + 短中长期目标 | 约 30% |

```text
1 分钟精简版删减策略：
  保留：专业背景 + 竞赛 + 项目 + 核心能力 + 绿盟/AI安全方向
  删除：运动细节、网球协会经历、ACG 兴趣
```

---

## 二、Part 1: Personal Introduction（个人介绍）

Good morning / afternoon. My name is Li Yuyang, and I am a Software Engineering major at `[University]`. During my time at university, I have built a solid foundation in software development and gained practical experience through coursework, competitions, and internships.

I have learned front-end frameworks such as React, Vue, and Three.js, as well as JavaScript, Spring Boot, C++, and Python—Python is the language I use most. I also participated in the Lanqiao Cup and the National College Student Mathematical Modeling Contest, where I won `[provincial / national]` third prizes in both.

Outside of academics, I enjoy jogging, fitness, tennis, table tennis, and badminton. I also served as a leader in our university tennis association, where I organized activities and coordinated members. My key strengths are reading comprehension, information extraction, clear communication, and fast cross-domain learning. I read academic English articles every day and enjoy classic literature, anime, manga, and light novels, which help me stay curious and open-minded.

I also joined an AI + front-end internship project, which I will explain in more detail in Part 2.

### 结构要点

| 信息块 | 表达内容 | 传递信号 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 身份 | 专业 + 学校（`[University]` 占位） | 科班背景 |
| 技术栈 | React/Vue/Three.js + JavaScript/Spring Boot/C++/Python | 前端为主、全栈视野 |
| 竞赛 | 蓝桥杯 + 数学建模（`[provincial / national]` 三等奖 × 2） | 量化能力证明 |
| 个人特质 | 网球协会负责人 + 每天读学术英文 + ACG 兴趣 | 领导力 + 好奇心 + 学习习惯 |
| 钩子 | 一句带出 AI + 前端实习项目 | 自然过渡到 Part 2 |

---

## 三、Part 2: Project Experience（项目经历）

In April, I joined a school-enterprise internship program and worked on an AI + front-end development project. I experienced the full software development lifecycle, from environment setup and coding to testing and deployment. We deployed the project on an Alibaba Cloud server so that users could access it in a real environment.

The biggest challenge was leading the team and motivating my teammates. At first, I felt nervous because it was my first time managing a team. I learned about teamwork and task assignment online, and then applied it by clarifying each member's responsibilities, setting milestones, and communicating regularly. As a result, our team finished the project two days ahead of schedule.

This experience taught me that software engineering is not only about coding; it depends on clear communication and task breakdown. It also made me realize that I want to work as a product manager, connecting technical feasibility, user needs, and team execution.

### 结构要点

| 故事线 | 表达内容 | 传递信号 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 背景 | 校企合作实习 + AI + 前端项目 | 有真实项目经历 |
| 全流程 | 环境搭建 → 编码 → 测试 → 部署（阿里云） | 了解完整生命周期 |
| 冲突 | 第一次带团队，紧张、缺乏经验 | 真实感，不粉饰 |
| 行动 | 自学团队管理 → 明确分工 → 设里程碑 → 定期沟通 | 学习能力 + 执行力 |
| 结果 | 提前两天交付 | 可量化的成果 |
| 收束 | 认识到 PM 的价值：连接技术、需求与执行 | 自然锚定岗位动机 |

## 四、Part 3: Industry Frontier & Career Direction（行业前沿与职业方向）

I am closely following the cybersecurity industry, especially the combination of AI and security. NSFOCUS is a good example: it is promoting both "AI for security" and "security for AI." The Fengyunwei platform uses AI to improve security operations, alert noise reduction, and automated response, while Qingfengwei focuses on protecting AI models and AI agents. I am also interested in emerging areas such as AI agent security, data security, and practical attack-defense.

In my view, the future of security products is not just adding AI features, but redesigning how products are built, delivered, and operated. My career goal is to become a B-end product manager who understands security scenarios, customer needs, and AI capabilities.

In the short term, I want to learn the full product lifecycle and understand industries such as telecom, finance, and energy. In the mid term, I hope to independently own a product line, especially in AI security. In the long term, I want to help define product direction and create real value for customers. That is why I am very interested in NSFOCUS. Thank you.

### 结构要点

| 层次 | 表达内容 | 传递信号 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 行业认知 | 关注 AI + 安全结合，点名绿盟双轮驱动 | 做过功课 |
| 产品理解 | 准确说出 Fengyunwei / Qingfengwei 的分工 | 研究过具体产品 |
| 视野 | AI agent 安全、数据安全、攻防实战 | 了解前沿方向 |
| 观点 | 安全产品未来是重构构建/交付/运营方式，而非堆 AI 功能 | 有独立判断 |
| 目标 | B 端 PM：懂安全场景 + 客户需求 + AI 能力 | 岗位画像匹配 |
| 路径 | 短（全流程+行业）→ 中（独立产品线）→ 长（定义方向） | 有规划、有野心 |

## 五、第一版主要问题与修改提示

| # | 原问题 | 修改方式 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | "Hello employer" 不自然 | 英文面试用 `Good morning / Good afternoon` |
| 2 | "篮球杯"应为"蓝桥杯" | 英文写作 `Lanqiao Cup` |
| 3 | "全国数学建模"表述模糊 | 写 `the National College Student Mathematical Modeling Contest (CUMCM)` |
| 4 | "stay three prize" 语法错误 | 改为 `won third prizes`；省级加 `provincial` |
| 5 | "wanna" 过于口语 | 面试中改为 `want to` 或 `would like to` |
| 6 | "soft engineering" 错误 | 改为 `software engineering` |
| 7 | "Dong business" 不确定公司名 | 说 `a school-enterprise internship program`；东软则用 `with Neusoft` |
| 8 | 重复词过多（secondly secondly、ability ability） | 精修版已合并 |
| 9 | 项目部分平淡 | 突出：**全流程、团队管理、沟通、任务拆解、提前两天完成**，自然衔接到 PM 岗位 |
| 10 | 第三部分缺功课感 | 必须提绿盟 **"AI for security" 与 "security for AI" 双轮驱动** |

## 六、练习建议

| 阶段 | 动作 | 标准 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 第一步 | 分三部分分别背熟 | 每部分独立脱稿 |
| 第二步 | 连起来录音一遍 | 总时长 **2.5–3 分钟** |
| 第三步 | 精听回放，修掉卡壳与语气词 | 表达自然、节奏稳定 |
| 1 分钟版 | 删掉运动细节和 ACG 兴趣 | 只留：专业 + 竞赛 + 项目 + 核心能力 + 绿盟/AI 安全 |

```text
面试当天注意：
  重点不是"背得完美"，而是让面试官听到四个信号——
  ① 你懂技术  ② 你能沟通  ③ 你能推进项目  ④ 你对 AI 安全方向有真实兴趣
```
